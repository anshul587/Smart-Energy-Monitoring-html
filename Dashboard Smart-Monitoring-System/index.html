About Smart Energy Monitoring System

About the Project

The Smart Energy Monitoring System & Suggestions for Energy Conservation is an industrial energy-monitoring platform that brings real-time visibility and predictive insight to electrical consumption across multiple circuits. ESP32-based nodes read PZEM energy meters and publish readings to a centralized store, where an AI backend analyses the data for anomalies, faults, peaks, forecasts, bill prediction and maintenance risk. A web dashboard presents everything live, and the integrated assistant, Ask BOB, answers questions in plain language. The system helps sites cut energy waste, catch faults early, and plan maintenance before failures occur.

Purpose

Centralized electrical monitoring gives operators a single, trustworthy view of where and how energy is used. By combining monitoring, analysis, prediction and conservation guidance, the system turns raw meter data into decisions: reducing waste, preventing downtime, and lowering bills without disrupting operations.

How It Works

  1. PZEM Energy Meters — measure voltage, current, power, energy, power factor and frequency on each circuit.
  2. ESP32 — reads the meters over the local bus and connects them to the network.
  3. Data Collection — readings are gathered and time-stamped continuously.
  4. Centralized Data Storage — all readings are stored in one place for live and historical access.
  5. AI Analysis — the backend runs detection and prediction on the stored data.
  6. Predictions & Recommendations — faults, peaks, forecasts, bill estimates and saving tips are produced.
  7. Dashboard / Reports / Ask BOB — results reach operators through live views, monthly reports, and the conversational assistant.

AI Intelligence

Anomaly detection learns normal meter behaviour and flags unusual patterns that may signal inefficiency or developing problems.

Fault diagnosis identifies abnormal electrical conditions such as over/under-voltage and over-current, associating each event with the affected PZEM.

Peak-load detection finds when and where demand is highest, helping avoid stress on the installation.

Predictive maintenance scores each meter for risk so attention can be planned before a failure, though it indicates risk rather than guaranteeing failure.

Power forecasting projects near-term demand from recent trends to support planning.

Bill prediction estimates upcoming cost from current consumption and tariff.

Energy-conservation suggestions turn analysis into actionable recommendations.

Ask BOB is the natural-language assistant that explains all of the above on demand.

Energy Conservation

The system analyses usage patterns and provides evidence-based suggestions covering peak-load reduction, idle consumption, high-power usage, high-current behaviour, power-factor conditions, and predictable high-load periods. All savings are presented as estimates derived from measured data, not guaranteed outcomes.

Fault & Alert System

The system detects abnormal electrical conditions and associates each detected event with the affected PZEM meter, so operators know exactly where attention is needed. Detected conditions are surfaced as warnings and, for the most serious cases, as emergency indicators within the dashboard. Indications reflect the analysed data; any required physical response depends on the site's own procedures.

Ask BOB

Ask BOB is the integrated AI assistant of the Smart Energy Monitoring System. It can answer project questions, team and guide questions, system-operation questions, and feature or advantage questions. It also answers live energy-data questions about faults, peaks, maintenance, forecasts, bill prediction and energy conservation.

Current Limitations

  • AI predictions and recommendations are estimates.
  • Long-term real-world data is required for final tuning and validation.
  • Predictive maintenance indicates risk and does not guarantee equipment failure.
  • Production deployment requires additional security hardening.
